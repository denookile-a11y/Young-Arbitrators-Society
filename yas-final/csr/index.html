YAS/CSR
Community & Social Responsibility

Access to justice, beyond the classroom.

Legal education, community outreach, and ADR awareness — where YAS puts advocacy into public service.

Our Impact

YAS's CSR programme extends arbitration and ADR literacy to communities with limited access to legal services — through legal education sessions, community outreach, and hands-on clinics run in partnership with established legal aid organisations.
